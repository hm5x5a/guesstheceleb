Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

At GuessTheCeleb.org, accessible from https://guesstheceleb.org, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use it.

No Account & No Personal Data Collection

Guess The Celeb is designed to operate without user accounts. We do not require you to register, submit names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Game progress, streaks, statistics, and settings are saved locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and are never sent to our servers.

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

We may display advertisements provided by reputable advertising networks (including Adsterra and Google AdSense) to keep our trivia game free for everyone. These third-party vendors use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

You may choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed instructions for cookie management can be found at your browser's respective website.

Log Files and CDN Analytics

Like most modern web platforms, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard anonymized server logs that include IP addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, and referring pages. These logs are used solely for security, DDoS mitigation, and server performance monitoring.

Children's Privacy

Guess The Celeb does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@guesstheceleb.org.
