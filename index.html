How it works

Each mode isolates one signature feature: hair, beard, nose or eyes. When Hair is picked, eyebrows are hidden. For Beard, the nose is hidden. For Eyes, forehead and nose are hidden.

A new puzzle unlocks every day for every mode, keeping its own streak. Solved puzzles reveal the full uncropped face!

Best Tips for Guessing

Strategies to Boost Your Win Streak

1. Look for Signature Hairlines & Fades

Notice distinctive tapers, buzzcuts, iconic dyed roots, or signature curls before making a guess.

2. Check Beard Density & Grooming

Pay attention to trimmed goatees, thick full beards, clean line-ups, or ginger stubble.

3. Focus on Eye Color & Iris Shape

Eye spacing and piercing iris colors (like Cillian Murphy's blue eyes) are instant dead giveaways.

4. Think of Global Pop Culture Icons

Puzzles feature globally famous figures in music, sports, and film. Start with recognizable A-list stars.

5. Try the Daily Mix Mode

In Daily Mix, each wrong guess reveals a different feature: Hair → Eyes → Nose → Beard → Full Face.

6. Common Nicknames & Aliases Work

Popular monikers like "Ye", "The Rock", "CR7", and "Messi" are accepted in addition to full names.

7. Practice Unlimited Mode

Play unlimited rounds with randomized celebrity crops to sharpen your visual recognition skills.

8. Stay Calm and Think Logically

Eliminate stars with different skin tones or facial structures to quickly narrow down your options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Guess The Celeb?
Guess The Celeb is a free daily pop-culture crop puzzle game where players identify world-famous celebrities from tightly cropped photos of their hair, beard, nose, or eyes. Inspired by viral TikTok street challenges, it brings the game to your browser with daily streaks and multiple modes.
How do I play?
Select your preferred mode from the top bar (Daily, Hair, Beard, Nose, Eyes, or Unlimited). Study the cropped photo, type your answer into the search bar, and submit. You have 5 tries to guess the mystery star.
What are the cropping rules?
Crops are strictly isolated to make the game challenging. When guessing by Hair, eyebrows are hidden. For Beard, the nose is hidden. For Eyes, forehead and nose are hidden. The puzzle remains tightly cropped so you rely on signature feature details.
How many attempts do I get?
You get 5 attempts per daily puzzle. After winning or giving up, the complete uncropped photo is revealed alongside your score distribution.
How does the Daily Streak work?
Solving any daily puzzle counts towards your consecutive streak. You can also track individual stats, win percentages, and try distributions in the Statistics (▥) panel.
Can I create my own crop games for TikTok, Reels, or parties?
Yes! Tap 'Custom' in the top navigation. Upload your own photos, drag crop boxes, and click 'Start run' to launch interactive Hold-to-Reveal Host Mode—perfect for recording street trivia videos or hosting party games.
Is Guess The Celeb free to play?
Yes, 100% free with no login and no account required. All photos and crops are processed locally on your device with complete privacy.
