Image Credits & Attribution

Guess The Celeb is committed to open culture, fair attribution, and intellectual property transparency. Because our visual trivia puzzles rely on real, world-famous personalities, all reference photographs are sourced from freely licensed public repositories.

Where Our Images Come From

Every celebrity portrait displayed in Guess The Celeb is sourced from the free media repository Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia. These photographs are published under Creative Commons licenses (primarily CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY 2.0) or have entered the Public Domain.

Transformative Educational Fair Use

Our game displays tight, isolated crops (e.g. hairline, eye contours, nasal bridges, or stubble) as visual clue puzzles for transformative trivia, educational testing, critique, and commentary. We never sell or license original high-resolution photos, and all cropped graphics are processed directly for the trivia guessing gameplay.
