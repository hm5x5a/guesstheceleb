How it works

Each mode isolates one signature feature: hair, beard, nose or eyes. When Hair is picked, eyebrows are hidden. For Beard, the nose is hidden. For Eyes, forehead and nose are hidden.

A new puzzle unlocks every day for every mode, keeping its own streak. Solved puzzles reveal the full uncropped face!

Daily Beard & Stubble Challenge

Mastering the Guess the celebrity by beard Mode

Step up to the Guess the Celebrity by Beard daily trivia challenge! Facial hair is one of Hollywood and pop culture’s greatest trademarks. From rugged stubble and trimmed goatees to full lumberjack beards and sharp jawline lineups, this mode challenges you to name the celebrity exclusively by their facial hair.

In beard mode, the upper face, eyes, and nose are completely cropped away. You are presented strictly with the jawline, chin, and mustache area. Study the color of the stubble, hair thickness, cheek line definition, and chin grooming to determine which A-list star is on screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play the beard version of Guess The Celeb?
Study the cropped beard or mustache shown on screen. Type the celebrity’s name or known alias into the search bar and submit. You have five attempts to guess correctly.

Why is the beard puzzle harder than hair?
Beard crops isolate only the lower jaw, chin, and mustache, hiding signature eyes and forehead structures. Identifying stars by stubble density and grooming lines requires sharp pop culture memory.

How many tries do I get for each beard puzzle?
You get 5 attempts per daily puzzle. After winning or giving up, the complete uncropped high-resolution photo is revealed.

When does the next beard puzzle unlock?
A new daily beard puzzle unlocks every night at midnight local time. All players worldwide receive the same celebrity puzzle on any given date.

Can I guess celebrities using their common nicknames?
Yes! Widely recognized aliases such as "Ye", "The Rock", "CR7", and "Messi" are accepted alongside full legal names.

Does it work smoothly on mobile phones?
Yes, the game is fully optimized for touchscreens, iPhones, and Android devices with zero downloads required.
