About Guess The Celeb

Guess The Celeb is a free, web-based daily pop culture trivia game created for fans of film, music, athletics, and digital culture. Inspired by viral TikTok street challenges, our game turns visual recognition into an engaging daily brain teaser where players identify famous figures from tightly cropped photos of their hair, beard, nose, or eyes.

Our Mission

We believe online games should be accessible, lightning fast, and privacy-first. Guess The Celeb requires no signups, no passwords, and collects zero personal user data. Everything—from image rendering to streak tracking—operates client-side directly on your device.

How Daily Puzzles Are Chosen

Every midnight (local time), a new set of puzzles unlocks across our distinct game modes: Hair, Beard, Nose, and Eyes. Puzzles are distributed deterministically worldwide using seeded cryptographic algorithms so you and your friends can compare guesses on an even playing field.

Image Licensing & Fair Use

All celebrity reference photos featured in Guess The Celeb are sourced from freely licensed Wikimedia Commons archives or educational public domain databases, complying with Creative Commons guidelines. We strictly crop images for educational trivia, commentary, and transformative gaming purposes under fair use doctrine. If you are a copyright holder with questions, please contact our team.
